NASA RAG Evaluation Dataset
=============================

Purpose:
Use these questions to test the NASA RAG chatbot and evaluate answer quality.
For each question, compare the chatbot response with the relevant retrieved
context from your NASA mission documents.

Questions
---------

1. What was Apollo 11?

Expected answer:
Apollo 11 was the first crewed NASA mission to land humans on the Moon.

2. Who were the astronauts on Apollo 11?

Expected answer:
The Apollo 11 crew consisted of Neil Armstrong, Buzz Aldrin, and Michael Collins.

3. Who was the first person to step onto the Moon during Apollo 11?

Expected answer:
Neil Armstrong was the first person to step onto the Moon.

4. What was the main objective of the Apollo 11 mission?

Expected answer:
The primary objective was to land astronauts on the Moon and safely return them to Earth.

5. What was Apollo 13?

Expected answer:
Apollo 13 was a NASA lunar mission that experienced a major in-flight emergency and did not complete its planned lunar landing.

6. What happened during the Apollo 13 mission?

Expected answer:
An oxygen-tank failure caused a serious spacecraft emergency, forcing the crew to abort the lunar landing and use the lunar module as a lifeboat.

7. Why is Apollo 13 considered a successful failure?

Expected answer:
Although the planned lunar landing was not completed, the crew survived and returned safely to Earth after extensive mission-control efforts.

8. What was the Challenger mission?

Expected answer:
Space Shuttle Challenger was a NASA Space Shuttle orbiter that flew multiple missions and was lost in the Space Shuttle Challenger disaster in 1986.

9. What happened to Space Shuttle Challenger?

Expected answer:
Challenger broke apart shortly after launch on January 28, 1986, resulting in the loss of all seven crew members.

10. How many crew members were aboard Challenger during the 1986 disaster?

Expected answer:
Seven crew members were aboard Challenger during the disaster.

11. Compare Apollo 11 and Apollo 13.

Expected answer:
Apollo 11 achieved the first crewed lunar landing, while Apollo 13 had to abort its lunar landing after a major spacecraft emergency and focused on returning its crew safely to Earth.

12. What role did mission control play in Apollo 13?

Expected answer:
Mission control worked with the astronauts to manage the emergency, develop procedures and solutions, conserve resources, and guide the spacecraft safely back to Earth.

13. What is a flight plan in the context of a NASA mission?

Expected answer:
A flight plan describes the planned sequence of mission activities, procedures, timelines, and operations for a spacecraft and its crew.

14. What types of documents are available in the NASA RAG dataset?

Expected answer:
The project dataset can contain mission transcripts, technical documents, audio transcriptions, flight plans and procedures, including text data associated with Apollo 11, Apollo 13, and Challenger.

15. Which NASA missions are represented in the project data?

Expected answer:
The project data is organized around Apollo 11, Apollo 13, and Challenger.

16. What is the purpose of retrieving documents before generating a response?

Expected answer:
Retrieved documents provide relevant source information that the language model can use to generate a grounded answer instead of relying only on its general knowledge.

17. What should the chatbot do when the answer is not available in the retrieved NASA documents?

Expected answer:
It should clearly state that the information is not available in the provided documents rather than inventing an answer.

18. What is RAGAS used for in this project?

Expected answer:
RAGAS is used to evaluate the quality of RAG responses using metrics related to response relevance, faithfulness, context precision, and other evaluation measures.

19. What is mission-specific filtering?

Expected answer:
Mission-specific filtering restricts document retrieval to a selected mission, such as Apollo 11, Apollo 13, or Challenger.

20. Why is conversation history useful in the NASA RAG chatbot?

Expected answer:
Conversation history allows the language model to understand previous questions and answers and maintain context across multiple turns.

Negative / Robustness Questions
-------------------------------

21. What was the landing site of Apollo 11?

Expected behavior:
Answer from the retrieved documents if the information is available. Do not invent
a landing site if it is not present in the retrieved context.

22. What was the exact temperature inside the Apollo 13 command module at a specific time?

Expected behavior:
Use the NASA documents if they contain the requested value. Otherwise, state
that the information is not available in the provided documents.

23. Who was the commander of a fictional NASA mission called Apollo 99?

Expected behavior:
State that the information is not available or that Apollo 99 is not represented
in the provided NASA documents. Do not fabricate an astronaut.

24. What was the Challenger mission's favorite color?

Expected behavior:
State that this information is not available in the provided mission documents.
Do not invent an answer.

Suggested Evaluation Procedure
------------------------------

For each question:

1. Enter the question into the Streamlit NASA RAG chatbot.
2. Record the generated answer.
3. Check the retrieved contexts shown/used by the application.
4. Compare the answer with the expected answer where applicable.
5. Check whether the answer is supported by the retrieved context.
6. Run RAGAS evaluation when enabled.
7. Record the returned metric scores.

Recommended metrics to observe:
- Faithfulness
- Response Relevancy
- Context Precision
- BLEU
- ROUGE

Note:
The expected answers above are reference answers for evaluation. Your actual
NASA documents should be treated as the authoritative source for document-grounded
answers. If a detail is absent from the documents, the chatbot should acknowledge
that rather than relying on an unsupported claim.
